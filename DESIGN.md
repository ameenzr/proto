# PROTO — From zero. To real.

## Direction
An independent technical studio with an editorial, industrial identity. The site pairs restrained engineering notation with expressive typography and an original parametric sculpture. Its story moves from possibility, to judgment, to tangible work, to the person behind it.

## Palette
- Canvas: charcoal `#131413`
- Surface: `#1b1c1a`
- Raised surface: `#242521`
- Primary text: warm off-white `#f0eee7`
- Secondary text: `#b4b4aa`
- Accent: orange `#ff7845`
- Contrast panels: sage `#c5cfb2`
- The alternate light theme uses warm drafting paper and a darker orange for contrast.

## Type and layout
Inter Tight provides oversized, tightly tracked display type and readable body copy. Martian Mono is reserved for technical annotations and section labels. Georgia italic gives the words “real” and “feeling” a human counterpoint.

The layout uses a 1440px maximum shell, fluid gutters, generous sectional spacing, and asymmetric content pairings. Use tonal changes to define large sections. Fine rules are reserved for navigation and engineering notation. Small radii keep the surfaces precise.

## Components
- Hero: original SVG torus generated from parametric geometry; draft/real buttons alter the density and geometry.
- Engagement: contrasting charcoal and sage cards with custom vector symbols.
- Work: five existing projects, with clearly labeled conceptual illustrations, not actual screenshots or verified product metrics. Native details disclosures preserve the context, decision, and outcome for each project.
- Studio: typographic monogram artwork rather than a placeholder or invented portrait.
- Field notes: unpublished pieces are explicitly marked as in the works.
- Contact: direct mail link and clipboard control with success/error feedback.

## Interaction and accessibility
Use semantic links and buttons, visible focus indicators, an accessible skip link, and a mobile menu with Escape dismissal and keyboard focus handling. Keep the theme available in the mobile menu. Persist preferences when storage is available without breaking the control when it is not.

Motion is slow and restrained. Honor reduced-motion preferences by disabling animation, transitions, and smooth scrolling. Keep essential content available without scroll-animation support. Do not add an animation or 3D library solely for decoration.
